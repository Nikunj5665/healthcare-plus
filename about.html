About us

We built HealthCare+ for wounds that take their time

A diabetic foot ulcer, a pressure sore or a leg ulcer changes so slowly that nobody can tell from one day to the next whether it's healing. That uncertainty is where harm happens.

Why we started

A foot ulcer that's left a few weeks too long can cost someone their foot, and a pressure sore can deepen in days. HealthCare+ exists to give people a clear, calm next step between visits. These four rules decide everything we build.

  • Clear over clever

    Every check ends with plain language and one clear next step, never medical jargon.

  • Careful by default

    When the answer isn't clear, HealthCare+ points people towards a doctor rather than away from one.

  • Private by design

    Photos of a wound are personal. They belong to the person who took them, and to nobody else.

  • Alongside doctors, never instead of them

    HealthCare+ helps people decide when to seek care and makes those visits easier.

Who it's for

Long-term wounds are mostly managed at home, by the person who has one or by someone close to them. These are the people we had in mind.

  1. 01

    People living with diabetes

    Checking your feet daily is the standard advice. Knowing what you're looking at, and when a small ulcer has become urgent, is the hard part.

  2. 02

    People recovering from surgery

    The weeks after you're discharged, when the dressing becomes your responsibility and the hospital is no longer down the corridor.

  3. 03

    People with limited mobility

    Anyone spending long hours in a bed or a chair, where a pressure sore can start quietly on a heel or a tailbone you can't easily see.

  4. 04

    People with leg ulcers

    Ulcers that come with swelling and compression bandaging, and have a habit of returning once they've healed.

  5. 05

    Family carers

    Looking after someone else's wound, often with no training, deciding alone whether today's change is worth a phone call.

  6. 06

    Community nurses

    A photo history the patient brings to the visit, so a fortnight of change can be seen at a glance instead of recalled.

Where we are today

We believe in being straight with people about what this does and doesn't do yet.

What works now

You can check a wound by answering a few questions about what kind it is, how long it's been open, how much it hurts and whether there are warning signs. You get one clear next step and wound-care guidance for that type of wound, and your checks are saved so you can follow the healing week by week.

What we're building

Analysis of the wound bed in the photo itself, wound size measured across weeks of photos, a report you can download for your wound clinic, and Tamil and Hindi versions of the whole app.

What it will never be

HealthCare+ will not diagnose conditions or prescribe treatment. Those are decisions for a registered doctor who can examine you in person.

Not yet reviewed by a clinician

Our wound-care steps and warning signs follow published guidance, but no doctor or tissue viability nurse has reviewed them yet. We would rather tell you that than imply a clinical sign-off we don't have. Treat HealthCare+ as a prompt to speak to your own clinician, never as a substitute for one. When a registered clinician has reviewed the content, their name, qualification and review date will replace this notice.
