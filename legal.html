Privacy policy

Where your information is kept

Everything you enter — your name, email address, the photos you upload and your answers — is stored in your own web browser, on the device you are using. It is not sent to us and it is not stored on our servers.

This has two consequences worth understanding. Your checks appear only on the device and browser you created them on, so you will not see them on a different phone or computer. And if you clear your browsing data, use private browsing or switch browsers, they are gone permanently and we cannot recover them for you.

How we use it

Only to give you guidance and to show your history of checks, on your own device. We cannot see your photos or answers. We don't sell your information, share it with advertisers or use it for marketing.

The contact form

The one thing that does reach us is a message you send through the contact form: your name, email address and what you write arrive in our email inbox, so that we can reply. Please don't include medical details or photos there.

Deleting your information

You are in control of it directly. Use Delete all my checks on your dashboard, or clear your browser's site data for this website. Either removes everything immediately. You don't need to ask us, and there is nothing held elsewhere for us to delete.

Keeping it safe

Photos of a wound are personal health information. Because they never leave your device, the main risk is someone else using that device. If you share a phone or computer, delete your checks when you have finished, and be aware that anyone who can unlock the device can open your history.

Terms of use

Guidance, not a diagnosis

HealthCare+ gives general guidance about long-term wound care, such as diabetic foot ulcers, pressure sores, leg ulcers and surgical wounds. It is not for fresh injuries such as cuts, burns, animal bites or stings. It does not diagnose conditions or prescribe treatment, and it doesn't replace your doctor or wound clinic. If you're worried about a wound, see a doctor.

Emergencies

Don't use HealthCare+ in an emergency. Call 112 or go to the nearest emergency department.

Photos of other people

Only upload photos of someone else if they're with you and agree to it.
