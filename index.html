Made for wounds that take weeks, not days

A long-term wound changes slowly, so it's hard to tell from one day to the next whether it's healing or getting worse. HealthCare+ follows it with you.

Diabetic foot ulcers

Ulcers on the sole, heel or toes, where lost feeling can hide the damage.

Pressure sores

Bedsores over the tailbone, hips, heels and elbows from sitting or lying too long.

Venous leg ulcers

Shallow, weeping ulcers on the lower leg, often with swelling and darkened skin.

Surgical wounds

Incisions, staples, drain sites and grafts you're looking after at home after an operation.

Wounds that have stalled

Anything still open after four weeks, or getting bigger instead of smaller.

Healing you want to track

Week-by-week photos that show whether a dressing or treatment is actually working.

Not for fresh injuries

HealthCare+ is for wounds you're managing over weeks. A new cut, a burn, an animal bite or a sting needs first aid and a doctor now, not a tracking app.

Some wound changes are an emergency

Black or dying tissue, a fever with shivering or confusion, red streaks spreading up the limb, or a limb that turns cold, blue or numb all need help right away.

Call 112

Questions people ask

Can't find your answer? Get in touch and we'll help.

Is this a medical diagnosis?

No. HealthCare+ gives you guidance on what to do next, such as carrying on with your care plan or getting the wound seen. It doesn't replace your doctor or wound clinic, and if you're worried, you should always see them.

Can I use it for a fresh cut, burn or animal bite?

No. HealthCare+ is built for wounds managed over weeks, such as diabetic foot ulcers, pressure sores, leg ulcers and surgical wounds. A new injury needs first aid and a doctor straight away, and an animal bite needs seeing the same day.

Who can see my photos?

Only you. Your photos are linked to your account, and you can delete any of them whenever you like.

What should I do if my result is red?

Get medical help straight away. Call 112 or go to the nearest emergency department. Don't wait to see whether it gets better.

How do I take a good photo?

Take it just after the dressing comes off, in daylight, from the same angle each time. Get close, hold your phone steady and place a coin next to the wound to show its size. The app shows these tips before you upload.

Can I check a wound for someone I care for?

Yes, as long as they're with you and agree to the photo. Many pressure sores and leg ulcers are looked after by a family member or carer, and the tracker is built with that in mind.
